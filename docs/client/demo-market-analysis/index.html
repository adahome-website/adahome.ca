Five reasons this range holds up

  1. The closest match just sold for $372,500.22 Somervale Point is also a half-duplex bungalow in Somer Pointe, 1,099 sq ft against 1,097 here, with 3 bedrooms. It sold on October 23 after 49 days, 7% under its $399,888 asking price.
  2. Fall buyers here are paying 7% under asking.Both Somer Pointe sales since September closed at 92–93% of their original price. Applied to this home, that points to about $364,000.
  3. Prices in the complex are below 2023–24 levels.22 Somervale Point sold for $405,500 in April 2023 and $372,500 now. 197 Somervale Point sold for $420,000 in January 2024 and $385,000 this month. Both are down about 8%.
  4. Higher asking prices aren't selling.61 Somervale Point has been listed for 86 days and cut twice, from $430,000 to $419,999. 118 Somervale Point was listed at $399,500 for 59 days this summer and came off the market without selling.
  5. The wider market is cooling.The South Calgary townhouse benchmark is down 5.6% since June, and Calgary has 4.45 months of townhouse supply, the most buyer-friendly it's been all year.

Why not lower? It has only been listed for 10 days, and sellers rarely accept a deep cut in the first few weeks. It also sits on a 6,167 sq ft lot, which the listing describes as one of the largest in the complex.

Everyday life

What life here looks like for the two of you

Somer Pointe is listed as an 18+ adult community inside Somerset, an established neighbourhood from the mid-1990s. Most of what you need day to day is within a few minutes.

Main-floor living

It's a bungalow, with the primary bedroom and a second bedroom on the main floor, according to the listing. Day-to-day life can happen on one level.

Errands in minutes

Shawnessy Town Centre has groceries, Walmart, Home Depot, banks, restaurants, coffee and a cinema.

Library and YMCA

Cardel Rec South at 333 Shawville Blvd SE has the Shawnessy YMCA and the Shawnessy Library under one roof.

Fish Creek Park

One of Canada's largest urban parks is next door, with paved pathways for gentle walks and bike rides, and Sikome Lake in summer.

Spruce Meadows

World-class show jumping just southwest of Somerset, plus its well-known Christmas market each winter.

Friendly community

The Somerset Bridlewood Community Association runs a community garden, Music in the Park and seasonal events, and family membership is $20.

Next step

Drafting your offer

When you're ready, I'll prepare the offer with you. Here's what goes into it, and why each piece matters.

Price

Price and deposit

My recommendation: we can start at $362,000, with room to move toward the target band if needed. The deposit is usually around $10,000 for homes at this price. Why it matters: a well-supported opening number keeps the seller negotiating, and a deposit shows you're serious.

Term

What's included

Appliances, window coverings and any furniture you'd like to stay with the home. Why it matters: only what's written in the offer is included, so anything you want to keep needs to be listed.

Term

Possession date

The seller lists possession as negotiable. Why it matters: a date that suits both sides can be worth as much to the seller as a higher price.

Condition

Financing

Gives your lender time to approve the mortgage on this home. Why it matters: if the financing doesn't come through, you can step away with your deposit protected.

Condition

Home inspection

A licensed inspector reviews the home's structure and systems. Why it matters: you learn what you're buying before you're committed, and can renegotiate or walk away if something serious comes up.

Condition

Condo document review

A review of the condo corporation's budget, reserve fund, bylaws and meeting minutes. Why it matters: in a condo you share costs and rules with your neighbours, so this shows any planned special assessments and rules on pets, age and rentals before you commit.
