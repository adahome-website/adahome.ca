About this home

A 2011 custom infill in Altadore with about 2,372 sq ft of finished space. The main floor is made for entertaining, with 9-foot ceilings, engineered hardwood, a formal dining room under a coffered ceiling, a living room with a gas fireplace, and a kitchen with an oversized island, walk-in pantry, granite counters and Viking and Miele appliances.

Upstairs are the primary suite with a walk-in closet and spa ensuite with heated floors, two more bedrooms, a full bath and laundry. A third-floor loft sits under a skylight, and the finished basement adds a rec room with a wet bar, a fourth bedroom and a bathroom. Outside: a full-width deck, a patio with an outdoor gas fireplace, gardens, and a heated double garage.
