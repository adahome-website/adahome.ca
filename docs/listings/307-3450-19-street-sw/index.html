About this home

A one-bedroom-plus-den condo in Avenue 33, a 2018 building steps from the coffee shops, restaurants and groceries of Marda Loop. The kitchen has quartz counters, lots of drawers and stainless steel appliances, and the living room has high ceilings, big windows and a door to a private balcony.

The bedroom has a walk-in closet and a window nook, and the bathroom has a deep soaker tub. The den works as a home office or a guest spot. Heated underground parking, a storage locker, in-suite laundry and central air are all included.
