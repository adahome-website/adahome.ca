Conditions · after your offer is accepted

What we'll check, and why it matters

Condition deadline: Fri Nov 6 (if accepted)

Deposit

~$10,000

Usual at this price. Paid in trust within a few days of acceptance, and it goes toward your purchase.

Financing

Your lender confirms the mortgage on this home. Why it matters: if it doesn't come through, you can step away with your deposit protected.

Home inspection

A licensed inspector reviews the structure and systems, about $500–700. Why it matters: you know what you're buying and can renegotiate or walk away if something serious comes up.

Condo document review

The budget, reserve fund, bylaws and minutes. Why it matters: it shows any planned special assessments and rules on pets, age and rentals before you commit.

Your to-dos in this step

Legal & paperwork · after conditions are removed

Signing with your lawyer

Signing: around Tue Dec 8
  1. Once conditions are removedI send the firm deal to your lawyer and your lender, so they can start their work.
  2. A few weeks beforeYour lender sends your mortgage instructions to your lawyer, and your lawyer reviews the title and condo details.
  3. About a week beforeYou meet your lawyer to sign the mortgage and transfer papers and hand over the rest of your down payment.
  4. Possession dayYour lawyer registers the home in your name at Alberta Land Titles and releases the funds, then I hand you the keys.

What to bring

  • Two pieces of ID, one with a photo
  • The rest of your down payment, sent the way your lawyer asks
  • Proof of home insurance starting on possession day

What you’ll sign

  • Your mortgage papers
  • The transfer of land into your name
  • The statement of adjustments, which splits property taxes and condo fees as of possession day

Good questions to ask

  • What is my final amount, and how should I send it?
  • Do you recommend title insurance?
  • When will the title be in my name?

Your to-dos in this step

Before you send any money, call your lawyer's office at a number you already know to confirm their banking details. Never trust payment instructions that arrive by email alone.
