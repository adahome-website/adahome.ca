About this home

A two-storey detached home on a large pie-shaped lot of about 8,200 sq ft, with mature trees and a treed bonus area behind the garage. The main floor has a living room with a fireplace, a dining room, the kitchen, a sunroom and a half bath, with three bedrooms and a full bath upstairs.

The finished basement adds a fourth bedroom, a rec room and storage, with a rough-in for another bathroom. Big-ticket updates are done: solar panels, a 2025 furnace, a tankless water heater, a new roof and eavestroughs, and 2021 siding. There is also a three-season screened porch and a double garage.
