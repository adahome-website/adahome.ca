About this home

A 2023 semi-detached home overlooking Poplar Park, with 2,527 sq ft over two storeys. The main floor is open from the living and family areas to a chef’s kitchen with a walk-in pantry, plus a powder room.

Upstairs, the primary suite has a spa-style ensuite, and both other bedrooms have their own ensuites, along with a laundry room and a flex lounge. The lower level is roughed in with in-floor heat, ready to finish your way. Out back are a deck and an insulated double garage off the lane, with the Bow River pathways and off-leash areas nearby.
