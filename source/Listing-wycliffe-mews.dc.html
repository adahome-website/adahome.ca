About this home

A 2018 custom home by Cook Custom Homes on about 0.8 acres in Watermark at Bearspaw, just west of Calgary. The main floor has a grand entry, a living room with a statement fireplace, a formal dining room, a private office, and a kitchen with a wine room, butler’s pantry and separate prep kitchen, with Miele, Sub-Zero and Wolf appliances.

Upstairs are a lounge with a wet bar and four bedrooms, each with an ensuite, including an owner’s retreat with a fireplace, dressing rooms and a balcony. The finished walkout level adds a bar, games room, gym, sauna, theatre and a fifth bedroom. Outside are decks with automated screens, a built-in BBQ, and a terrace with a swim spa and fire pit.
