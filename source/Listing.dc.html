About this home

A detached family home on a tree-lined West Hillhurst street, with 1,862 sq ft above grade plus a finished basement. The upper floor has vaulted ceilings and skylights, and the main floor flows from the front living room to a bright kitchen with an island and the dining area.

Recent updates include new front stucco and fresh paint, and the detached double garage has a 240V outlet ready for an electric vehicle. You're minutes from the Bow River pathways, Kensington and the Foothills Medical Centre.
